If you're old enough to remember the Kingdome, the center of all things Seattle Sports, and you're old enough to remember Tecmo Super Bowl, Ken Griffey Jr. Presents Major League Baseball (fun to play, painful to say), NBA Jam and the rest, then you're the kind of person I want to show up here. 1995 was a great year for the Mariners, the unforgettable call being made by the legendary Dave Niehaus. 1977 was a great year for the Sonics, winning their first championship and becoming the first Seattle sports team of the Big 4 sports to bring a title home. 2013 Seemed a long time ago, too, when the Seahawks brought their first and our second title home to Seattle. 

There's also a lot of heartache in Seattle sports, though. We lost our beloved Sonics in what can only be described as Hardwood Robbery. We lost our greatest player ever to a trade when it seemed like he'd be a Mariner for life. We suffered the longest playoff drought (again, of the Big 4 sports) in history for 21 years. But through it all, the general fanbase in Seattle is likely the most unique and, to my utterly biased opinion, the best out there. We're definitely the most resilient. 

This website, this whole project (really) of mine is a tribute to the fans of Seattle sports and the history and culture we lived and continue to endure/enjoy, depending on who you're talking to. We got a hockey team now, one that I think has a good future. We just need the NBA to give us our Sonics back. There is hope.

I combined my great love of Seattle sports with the childhood love of video games. I say childhood, but we all know it continues to this day. Primarily, my platforms were the famous and trusty NES and SNES, respectively. Boasting great games such as the aforementioned Tecmo Super Bowl, NBA Jam and KGJPMLB (I'm not typing all that again.), these two platforms produced a myriad of historically good (and hard) games. 

This is an outlet for me. Like everyone else out there, we're all busy with the every day hustle and bustle. And while I have those same commitments to faith, family and employment, having something to do like this provides me some casual down time. I want to stay from the start I'm here to have fun with this. I enjoy writing and will, from time to time, contribute to this little digital newspaper as I see fit. Maybe something comes of it, maybe not. Maybe I just get to have some fun with it and learn a few skills. And maybe, you get to be entertained. I hope so. 

So, subscribe (to the YouTube channel) and bookmark this site so you can follow along. Let's rewind, relive and remake Seattle Sports history, together.
